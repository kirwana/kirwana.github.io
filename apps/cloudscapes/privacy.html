CloudScapes privacy

For the CloudScapes watch face and the CloudScapes App for Garmin watches, by JumpHour. Last updated 6 November 2026.

What is sent

To show the forecast, the watch sends its location, rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km), to the CloudScapes server. The request goes through the Garmin Connect app on your phone. Nothing else is sent: no name, account, email, device ID or health data. As with any internet request, the server also sees the network address it came from.

What the server does with it

  • It rounds the location again, to about 11 km, and asks Open-Meteo for the weather forecast and WorldTides for the tides at that rounded spot.
  • It keeps the forecast for that 11 km area in memory for up to 30 minutes (tides up to 6 hours), so nearby requests share it, then forgets it.
  • The server runs on Microsoft Azure (Australia East). Azure's standard request logs, which include the rounded location, are kept for up to 90 days for fixing problems, then deleted automatically.

What we don't do

We don't sell, share or use your location for anything except returning your forecast. There is no tracking, advertising or analytics in the watch face or the app.

On your watch

The last location and forecast are kept on the watch, in the face's and the app's own storage, until the next update or until you remove them from the watch.
