An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A dramatic black and white coastal photograph capturing sandstone cliffs rising above a misty ocean. Layered rock formations catch the fading light while a soft haze rolls across the horizon, blurring the line between sea and sky. Waves crash quietly at the base of the cliffs, adding a sense of movement to the still, moody scene.

The high contrast tones and timeless monochrome palette make this print a striking addition to any coastal, minimalist, or modern interior. It brings a quiet, contemplative energy to living rooms, bedrooms, offices, or reading nooks, pairing beautifully with neutral color schemes and natural textures like wood and linen.

This atmospheric seascape works well as a solo statement piece or as part of a curated gallery wall alongside other nature and travel photography. It also makes a thoughtful gift for anyone who loves the ocean, dramatic landscapes, or classic black and white imagery.
