An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A vivid amaryllis flower fills this photographic card, its orange and cream petals streaked with deep red veins and lit by warm natural sunlight. Slender green leaves and unopened buds surround the bloom, capturing the lush energy of a garden in full flower.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the same amaryllis awaits your own handwritten note, leaving plenty of room for a personal message. This design is not tied to a single occasion, making it a versatile choice for birthdays, thank you notes, get well wishes, or just because.

Perfect for gardeners, flower enthusiasts, and anyone who appreciates the beauty of botanical photography, this card brings a bright, cheerful touch to any greeting. Its bold colors and detailed petals make it a striking way to brighten someone's day.
