An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This printable greeting card features a close-up photograph of a delicate pink daisy in full bloom, its golden center visited by a striped hoverfly caught mid-pollination. The soft green background and bright petals bring a cheerful, garden-fresh feel to any occasion.

Inside the card is a light pencil sketch of the same charming hoverfly and flower, leaving plenty of room for your own personal handwritten message. This makes it a wonderful choice for birthdays, thank you notes, get well wishes, or simply to brighten someone's day for no particular reason at all.

Perfect for nature lovers, gardeners, and anyone who appreciates the small wonders of the natural world, this card brings a touch of macro photography beauty to your correspondence. Send a little sunshine and a bit of buzzing garden charm to someone special.

What you receive

The card

A 150 mm square (5.9 in) card, folded at the side, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. At home it prints at 136 mm square (5.4 in) on A4 and 127 mm square (5 in) on US Letter, a little smaller than full size, because opened out it is wider than the paper; a print shop prints the full 150 mm square (5.9 in).

The qr code on the back

The back of the card has a small QR code. Scanning it opens this same photograph as a large wall print in the shop: Petals in Light Set of 4, a digital download to print at home or at a print shop, at sizes for framing. A nice extra for whoever receives the card.

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 150 mm square (5.9 in) folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
