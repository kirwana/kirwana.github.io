An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This printable greeting card features a close-up photograph of a delicate pink daisy in full bloom, its golden center visited by a striped hoverfly caught mid-pollination. The soft green background and bright petals bring a cheerful, garden-fresh feel to any occasion.

Inside the card is a light pencil sketch of the same charming hoverfly and flower, leaving plenty of room for your own personal handwritten message. This makes it a wonderful choice for birthdays, thank you notes, get well wishes, or simply to brighten someone's day for no particular reason at all.

Perfect for nature lovers, gardeners, and anyone who appreciates the small wonders of the natural world, this card brings a touch of macro photography beauty to your correspondence. Send a little sunshine and a bit of buzzing garden charm to someone special.
