An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A lone sea rock stands anchored in swirling sand as long exposure waves blur into silk around it, while a soft lavender and gold sunrise glows on the horizon behind distant cliffs. This tranquil coastal photograph captures the quiet drama of shoreline light and motion.

Inside the card is a light pencil sketch of the same rock scene, leaving space for your own personal note, making it a versatile choice for birthdays, thank yous, thinking of you, or simply saying hello. It suits nature lovers, beach wanderers, photographers, and anyone who appreciates a peaceful seaside view.

A thoughtful, ready to personalize card for coastal souls and calm-seeking hearts alike, perfect for marking any occasion with a touch of ocean serenity.

What you receive

Instant digital download, five files. Nothing is posted to you.

The card

A 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape card, folded along the top, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

The qr code on the back

The back of the card has a small QR code. Scanning it opens this same photograph as a large wall print in the shop: Long Exposure Ocean Print, a digital download to print at home or at a print shop, at sizes for framing. A nice extra for whoever receives the card.

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
