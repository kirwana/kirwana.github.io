An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features a striking black and white photograph of a raven perched on a weathered tree stump, its sharp eye and curved beak captured in fine detail against a softly blurred forest background. The textured bark and moody lighting give the image a timeless, painterly quality that celebrates the quiet power of this often misunderstood bird.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the raven echoes the cover art, leaving space for your own personal message. Its versatile design suits many occasions, from a birthday or thank you note to a simple just because card for the nature lover, bird watcher or animal photography enthusiast in your life.

Whether sent to a fellow birder or someone who loves striking wildlife imagery, this card offers a memorable way to share a moment of quiet, wild beauty.
