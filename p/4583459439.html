An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A vivid red azalea blossom fills this card with warmth, its ruffled petals glowing in bright sunlight and delicate stamens reaching outward. The close-up view captures the rich texture and color of the flower, with soft green foliage blurred gently behind it, giving the image a natural, sunlit garden feel.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the same flower awaits, leaving room for your own handwritten note. This card suits birthdays, thank you notes, get well wishes, or simply a just because moment shared with someone who loves flowers and the outdoors.

Perfect for gardeners, nature lovers, or anyone who appreciates the beauty of blooming flowers, this card brings a bright and cheerful touch to any occasion, offering a thoughtful and personal way to brighten someone's day with vibrant color.
