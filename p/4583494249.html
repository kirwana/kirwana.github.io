A solitary tree stands silhouetted against a sweeping sky ablaze with gold, peach and soft blue clouds at sunset. Rolling hills and distant storm clouds add depth to this warm, atmospheric landscape photograph, capturing that quiet, breathtaking moment when day turns to dusk.

Inside is a light pencil sketch of the same lone tree, leaving plenty of room for your own handwritten message. Because it is left open ended, this card suits nearly any occasion, a birthday, thank you note, thinking of you, encouragement or just because.

It is especially fitting for nature lovers, photography enthusiasts and anyone who finds peace in wide open skies and golden light. Send a bit of calm, natural beauty to someone who will appreciate a quiet moment captured in color.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

What you receive

Instant digital download, five files. Nothing is posted to you.
- Home print PDFs for A4 and US Letter, with cut and fold marks
- A print-shop PDF at full size with 1/8 in (3.2 mm) bleed
- JPG images for online card printers: the front, inside and back as separate images, as most card-printing sites ask for, plus the flat sheets
- A one-page printing guide

The card

A 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape card, folded along the top, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
