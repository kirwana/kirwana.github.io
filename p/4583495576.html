This card features an original photograph of a glowing sunrise breaking over the ocean, with waves cascading over rocky tide pools in the foreground. Warm golden light meets cool blue water in a peaceful, uplifting scene that captures the quiet power of the sea at dawn.

Inside you will find a light pencil sketch of the same coastal scene along with open space to write your own personal message, making this card suitable for any occasion, from birthdays and thank yous to encouragement or just because.

It is a wonderful choice for nature lovers, beach enthusiasts, and anyone who finds calm in the rhythm of the ocean. The dramatic sunrise colors and flowing water make it a striking, memorable card that stands out from typical greeting cards, whether given to a friend, family member, or coworker who appreciates the beauty of the natural world.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

What you receive

The card

A 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape card, folded along the top, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

The qr code on the back

The back of the card has a small QR code. Scanning it opens this same photograph as a large wall print in the shop: Golden Hour Ocean Print, a digital download to print at home or at a print shop, at sizes for framing. A nice extra for whoever receives the card.

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
