A printable photo calendar of original photographs by Alan and Carolina: one photograph a month, printed at home or at a print shop.

Water Study is a year of original black and white photographs of the sea, lakes and shoreline. Old jetties stretch out over still tidal pools, pebble beaches meet the water under heavy skies, and long exposures smooth calm water into soft silver. Each month has one large photograph that brings a quiet, reflective mood to the wall above a desk or in a kitchen or hallway. The grid starts on Monday and marks Australian national holidays, so it suits anyone living in Australia. It makes a thoughtful gift for lovers of the coast, minimalist decor and monochrome fine art photography, and for anyone who wants a calm, uncluttered year.
