This card features a close-up photograph of a sunflower in full bloom, its golden petals glowing against a clear blue sky. The rich detail of the seed-filled center and warm amber tones capture the bright, cheerful spirit of summer.

Inside is a light pencil sketch of the sunflower, leaving plenty of room for your own handwritten message. Its warm and sunny character makes it a lovely choice for a birthday, a thank you note, a just because card, or any moment you want to send a little sunshine to someone special.

This design suits nature lovers, gardeners, and anyone who appreciates the beauty of flowers photographed in natural light. The simple, timeless composition works well for both casual and heartfelt greetings, making it a versatile card to keep on hand for any occasion throughout the year.

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

The card

A 150 mm square (5.9 in) card, folded at the side, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. At home it prints at 136 mm square (5.4 in) on A4 and 127 mm square (5 in) on US Letter, a little smaller than full size, because opened out it is wider than the paper; a print shop prints the full 150 mm square (5.9 in).

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 150 mm square (5.9 in) folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
