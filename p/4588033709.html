A lone volcano rises above a silent salt flat on the Bolivian Altiplano in this black and white landscape photograph. Its snow-dusted summit catches the high-altitude light against a deep, darkening sky, while the cracked mineral crust of the plain leads the eye from swirling dark channels in the foreground towards small white mounds and the distant peak.

The monochrome treatment strips the scene back to form, texture and tone, giving it a quiet, timeless feel that recalls classic fine art landscape photography. Rich blacks and soft silvery greys make it a calm, grounding presence on the wall.

Hang it in a living room, study or hallway where a minimalist or modern interior needs a strong focal point, or pair it with other monochrome prints for a gallery wall. It sits comfortably alongside neutral palettes, natural wood, concrete and linen. A thoughtful gift for travellers, hikers, mountain lovers and anyone who has fallen for the wild, empty spaces of South America.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

What you receive

Instant digital download. High resolution JPG files at 300 DPI covering a wide range of print sizes, in both inches and centimetres. Nothing is posted to you.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
