An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A vivid California poppy opens fully to the sun, its golden orange petals glowing against a soft green garden backdrop. Captured in close, sunlit detail, this original photograph celebrates the simple beauty of a single wildflower in bloom.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the poppy echoes the cover, leaving plenty of room for your own handwritten note. The blank interior makes this card wonderfully versatile, perfect for birthdays, thank you notes, encouragement, or just because.

Ideal for gardeners, nature lovers, and anyone who appreciates botanical beauty, this card brings a bright and cheerful moment to whoever receives it. Its warm colors and natural charm suit both casual and heartfelt occasions, making it a lovely keepsake for friends and family who love flowers and the outdoors.
