A dramatic black and white photograph of the Inca citadel at Pisac, high above the Sacred Valley in Peru. Ancient stone walls sit on top of a steep hill, and terraced steps climb toward them from the foreground. Heavy clouds roll across the sky and the rugged Andes rise behind, giving the scene a timeless, almost cinematic weight.

The monochrome finish brings out every texture: the fitted stonework, the curve of the farming terraces, the wild scrub on the slopes and the folds of the distant mountains. It is a picture about history, endurance and the quiet grandeur of the high Andes.

This print suits a modern living room, a study or a hallway gallery wall, where its strong contrast works well with neutral, industrial or minimalist interiors. It sits comfortably beside other black and white photography and adds a sense of travel and adventure to an office or reading nook. It makes a thoughtful gift for anyone who has walked the Sacred Valley, dreams of visiting Peru, or loves archaeology, ancient civilisations and mountain landscapes.
