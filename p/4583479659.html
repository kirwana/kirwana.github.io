An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original photograph of a dramatic coastal seascape at sunset. Golden light breaks through moody storm clouds along the horizon while a rugged headland rises above the shore. In the foreground, weathered rock formations sit amid the soft, silky blur of ocean waves rushing over them, captured with a long exposure that gives the water a dreamy, misty quality.

The dramatic sky and textured rocks make this a striking choice for anyone who loves the ocean, coastal landscapes, or fine art nature photography. It suits a birthday, thank you note, congratulations, or just because message to a beach lover, hiker, or photography enthusiast. Inside, a light pencil sketch of the same scene invites a heartfelt handwritten note, making it a versatile card for nearly any occasion or recipient who appreciates natural beauty.
