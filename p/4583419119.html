An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original close-up photograph of a vivid yellow marigold in full bloom, its ruffled petals glowing in warm sunlight against a soft green garden backdrop. The rich detail and cheerful color make it a lovely way to bring a touch of nature to someone's day.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the same flower awaits, along with plenty of blank space for your own personal message. Because the inside is left open, this card suits almost any occasion: a birthday, a thank you note, get well wishes, or simply a just because hello to someone who loves flowers and gardens.

Perfect for gardeners, nature lovers, and anyone who appreciates a bright, botanical greeting made from a genuine photograph rather than a generic illustration.
