An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A weathered steam locomotive rests in a vast desert graveyard, its rusted iron hull weathered by decades of sun and wind. Captured in striking black and white, this image reveals the raw texture of rivets, bolts, and corroded steel against the soft, sweeping lines of distant mountains and open sky.

This piece captures the haunting beauty of industrial decay, a reminder of ambition and abandonment set within nature's quiet vastness. The dramatic contrast and moody tones make it a striking addition to any space seeking depth and character.

Perfect for a study, office, living room, or industrial-style loft, this print brings a sense of history and rugged elegance to your walls. It pairs beautifully with modern minimalist interiors as well as rustic or vintage themed rooms.

A thoughtful gift for train enthusiasts, photographers, or lovers of desolate landscapes and industrial history. This artwork invites viewers to pause and reflect on time, decay, and the passage of eras long gone.
