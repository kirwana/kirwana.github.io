Towering green cliffs plunge into a churning Atlantic sea beneath a moody, cloud-swept sky. This striking image of the Cliffs of Moher captures the raw power of the Irish coastline, with layered rock faces, wild grasses, and rolling whitecaps stretching toward the horizon.

The rich greens and steely blue-greys create a naturally soothing yet dramatic palette that works beautifully in living rooms, offices, reading nooks, or entryways. It brings a sense of adventure and open air into any space, whether displayed alone as a bold statement piece or paired with other coastal and landscape prints in a gallery wall.

Perfect for lovers of Ireland, travel photography, and rugged natural landscapes, this piece suits modern, coastal, farmhouse, or minimalist interiors alike. It also makes a thoughtful gift for hikers, nature enthusiasts, or anyone who dreams of standing at the edge of the world watching the waves roll in.

Bring the wild beauty of the Irish coast into your home with this evocative cliffside seascape.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
