A pair of black and white photographs of Machu Picchu, sold as a set of 2 prints designed to hang together. They share the same shape and scale, so they line up neatly side by side or stacked on a wall.

Together these prints explore the ancient Inca citadel high in the Peruvian Andes, rendered in rich monochrome. Steep forested peaks become deep charcoal, weathered stone terraces turn pale silver, and light falls across ruin and mountain in soft, dramatic contrast. The palette is restrained and timeless, letting texture, scale and shadow carry the mood: quiet, grand and a little mysterious.

The set suits a living room, study, office or hallway where you want a calm but striking focal point. Their neutral tones sit easily with modern, minimalist, industrial or Scandinavian interiors, and they pair well with natural wood, linen and black metal. As a matched set they bring a considered gallery feel to a wall without competing with the rest of the room.

They make a thoughtful gift for travellers, hikers, history lovers and anyone who has walked the Inca Trail or dreams of doing so one day.

About the photograph

Original photographs by Alan and Carolina, taken with real cameras. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

What you receive

Instant digital download. High resolution JPG files at 300 DPI covering a wide range of print sizes, in both inches and centimetres. Nothing is posted to you.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
