An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A close-up photograph of a delicate pansy blossom, its purple, white, and golden-orange petals softly lit against a lush green garden background. The delicate veining and velvety texture of the petals are beautifully captured, celebrating the simple beauty of nature.

This card suits many occasions, from a cheerful birthday greeting to a heartfelt thank you or simply a just-because note to brighten someone's day. It is a lovely choice for gardeners, flower lovers, or anyone who appreciates the quiet charm of blooming things.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the pansy awaits your own personal message, making this card a thoughtful and creative way to reach out to friends and family for any occasion that calls for a touch of natural beauty.
