Water Study is a 2027 calendar of original black and white photographs of still water: a long pier with its reflection on a calm shore at dusk, smooth long exposure lakes, pebble beaches and moody skies over the sea. The mood is quiet and calm, with soft greys and clean lines that make a peaceful year on the wall. It suits fans of minimalist decor, fine art and coastal photography, and it makes a thoughtful gift for a home office, a new apartment or anyone who loves the sea. Each month has a large photograph above a clean grid that starts on Sunday and marks US federal holidays.
