An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A tranquil black and white photograph of a weathered wooden pier stretching into calm coastal waters, its reflection mirrored in the still shallows below. Soft clouds drift across a moody sky, while scattered rocks and gentle tide pools add quiet texture to the scene. The pier's old lampposts and a small shelter at its end evoke a sense of timeless stillness and peaceful solitude.

This card suits nature lovers, beach wanderers and anyone who appreciates classic coastal scenery. It works beautifully as a thank you card, a just because note, or a thoughtful hello for someone who loves the sea and quiet places.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the pier awaits your own handwritten message, leaving room for birthdays, encouragement, sympathy or simple friendly greetings. A versatile, elegant design fit for any occasion where a calm, artistic touch is welcome.
