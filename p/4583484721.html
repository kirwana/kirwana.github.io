A dramatic coastal sunrise fills this photograph, with brilliant orange and gold light breaking through heavy clouds above a rugged headland. In the foreground, ocean waves rush and swirl over dark rock pools, blurred by a long exposure into soft, silky ribbons of color that mirror the fiery sky above.

This card suits nature lovers, beach wanderers, and anyone who finds peace by the sea. Its warm, hopeful glow makes it a lovely choice for a birthday, a thank you note, an encouraging just because message, or simply to let someone know you are thinking of them.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the same coastal scene leaves plenty of room for your own handwritten words, making this a thoughtful and personal card for any occasion that calls for a touch of natural beauty.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

The card

A 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape card, folded along the top, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

The qr code on the back

The back of the card has a small QR code. Scanning it opens this same photograph as a large wall print in the shop: Fiery Sunset Seascape Print, a digital download to print at home or at a print shop, at sizes for framing. A nice extra for whoever receives the card.

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
