Andean Silver is a pair of black and white photographs of the high Andes of Peru, sold as a set of 2 prints designed to hang together. They share the same shape and scale, so they line up neatly side by side or stacked on a wall.

Both prints take in the same rugged mountain country: jagged dark ridges, snow caught in the high gullies, and bright clouds drifting over the peaks. The monochrome treatment holds them together as one body of work. Deep blacks, crisp whites and a full range of silver greys bring out the texture of rock, scree and thin mountain trails, and the light gives them a quiet, dramatic mood.

As a pair, these prints bring a sense of space and stillness to a room. They suit a living room, study, hallway or bedroom, and their neutral black and white palette sits easily with modern, Scandinavian, industrial or rustic interiors. Hang them over a sofa, a desk or a console table for a calm, balanced display.

They make a thoughtful gift for hikers, climbers and travellers, or for anyone who loves Peru, the Andes and mountain landscapes.
