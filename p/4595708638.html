A printable coloring book of original photographs by Alan and Carolina, each one drawn as a page to colour, with the photographs at the back to colour from.

Big Bold Flowers is a printable coloring book of 18 pages, each drawn from a real photograph of a flower in bloom. You will find daisy-like blooms, pansies, violas and more, outlined in big, clear shapes with open petals that are easy to fill with colored pencils, markers or paint. The original photographs are included at the back, so you can color from life or choose your own palette. It suits adult colorers, gardeners, flower lovers and anyone who wants a gentle, screen-free break. Settle in with a cup of tea and let the slow, quiet rhythm of coloring each petal help you unwind.

The book

18 coloring pages, each drawn from one of my own photographs, one page a sheet, with a cover. At the back, a colour reference for every page: the page coloured in flat, shape by shape, with its photograph and its palette of colours, named. Paper sizes: US Letter and A4, at 300 dpi.

Printing
