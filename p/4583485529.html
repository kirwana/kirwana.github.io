This card features an original photograph of the famous Moeraki Boulders scattered along a misty New Zealand beach. Giant spherical stones rest on wet sand as gentle waves roll in, with a fog-shrouded cliff line fading into the distance. The soft, moody light and quiet coastal scene give the image a calm, contemplative feel.

Inside is a light pencil sketch of the boulders, along with open space for your own handwritten message, making this card suitable for any occasion you choose to make it. It works beautifully as a birthday card, a thank you note, a sympathy card, or simply a just because greeting for someone who loves the ocean, travel, geology, or nature photography.

A thoughtful choice for beach lovers, hikers, geologists, or anyone drawn to unique natural landscapes and dramatic coastal scenery.

The card

A 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape card, folded along the top, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

The qr code on the back

The back of the card has a small QR code. Scanning it opens this same photograph as a large wall print in the shop: Moeraki Boulders Print, a digital download to print at home or at a print shop, at sizes for framing. A nice extra for whoever receives the card.

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
