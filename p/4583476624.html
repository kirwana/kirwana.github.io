An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features a close up photograph of cascading wisteria blossoms, their pale lilac petals catching soft sunlight against a blurred backdrop of green foliage. The delicate clusters hang like natural chandeliers, capturing the gentle beauty of a garden in full spring bloom.

Perfect for anyone who loves flowers, gardens or nature photography, this card suits birthdays, thank you notes, get well wishes or simply saying you are thinking of someone. The soft, romantic tones make it especially fitting for spring celebrations, or as a thoughtful gift for a gardener or nature lover in your life.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the wisteria awaits your own personal message, leaving room to color it in or simply write your note as is. A versatile, elegant design suited to many occasions throughout the year.
