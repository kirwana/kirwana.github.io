A printable photo calendar of original photographs by Alan and Carolina: one photograph a month, printed at home or at a print shop.

Spend 2027 among the wide, quiet landscapes of Bolivia. The cover shows the Uyuni salt flat, its white hexagon crust running out to a lone volcano under a deep blue sky.
