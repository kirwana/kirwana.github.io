A lone pink flamingo wades through the still, mirror-like waters of a high altitude salt lagoon, framed by golden grasses in the foreground and distant purple volcanic peaks under a clear blue sky. Captured in the remote highlands of Bolivia, this image balances quiet solitude with striking natural color, from the warm tones of the reeds to the cool blues of the water and sky.

This print brings a sense of calm, wide open space into any room. Its serene palette and simple composition make it a natural fit for a living room, bedroom, office, or reading nook, especially in spaces styled with earthy, minimalist, or boho influences. The wildlife subject adds a touch of gentle interest without overwhelming a wall, and its horizontal format works beautifully as a single statement piece or as part of a gallery wall grouping with other nature photography.

A thoughtful gift for travelers, birdwatchers, or anyone drawn to landscapes that feel both vast and peaceful. This listing is for a digital file only, ready for you to print and display at home.
