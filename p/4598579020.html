Machu Picchu in Monochrome is a pair of black and white photographs of the ancient Inca citadel high in the Peruvian Andes, sold as a set of 2 prints designed to hang together. They share the same shape and scale, so they line up neatly side by side or stacked on a wall.

Together these prints capture the drama of the place: steep peaks rendered in deep charcoal, weathered stone terraces, and soft mountain light drifting through the haze. Without colour, what stays is texture, contrast and atmosphere, which gives the set a timeless, documentary feel.

They suit a living room, study, hallway or office where you want something calm but striking. The neutral black and white palette works with modern, Scandinavian, industrial and rustic interiors alike, and sits comfortably beside wood, linen and stone. As a pair they make an easy centrepiece for a gallery wall or a balanced feature above a sofa, bed or console.

A thoughtful gift for travellers, hikers, history lovers and anyone who has walked the Inca Trail or dreams of it.

About the photograph

Original photographs by Alan and Carolina, taken with real cameras. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

What you receive

Instant digital download. High resolution JPG files at 300 DPI covering a wide range of print sizes, in both inches and centimetres. Nothing is posted to you.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
