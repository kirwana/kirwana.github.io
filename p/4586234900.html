An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A small branch reaches across a soft, blurred green background. It holds four glowing crimson leaves, and the largest is a perfect natural heart. Light shines through the leaves and shows every fine vein in deep reds and warm orange. The result is a quiet, simple image full of warmth.

This original photograph shows a heart that nature made, which suits Valentine's Day, anniversaries and love notes. It also works for a simple thinking of you or just because message to someone special. Gardeners, nature lovers and anyone who loves autumn colour will enjoy it too.

Inside is a light pencil sketch of the leaves with plenty of room for your own words. Because the card is blank, it fits a birthday, a thank you or a quiet note of affection.
