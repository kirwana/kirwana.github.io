A printable coloring book of original photographs by Alan and Carolina, each one drawn as a page to colour, with the photographs at the back to colour from.

Ten peaceful coloring pages of shorelines, still lakes and old piers. Walk out along a wooden jetty into misty lake water, color a white lighthouse above a rocky harbour, bring a long seaside pier and its reflection to life, and shade chalk cliffs rising over a quiet bay. Each page is drawn from a real photograph, and the original photos are included at the back so you can match the true colors of sky and sea or invent your own. A lovely choice for anyone who loves the coast, lakeside walks or travel memories, and a calm, mindful way to slow down for an hour with your pencils.

The book

10 coloring pages, each drawn from one of my own photographs, one page a sheet, with a cover. At the back, a colour reference for every page: the page coloured in flat, shape by shape, with its photograph and its palette of colours, named. Paper sizes: US Letter and A4, at 300 dpi.

Printing

Print the pages you want at actual size, one side only, on heavier paper or card for pencils and paint. Print a page again whenever you want another go.
