An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original photograph of a soft, misty waterfall spilling over dark, moss covered rocks into a quiet forest pool. Delicate leaves frame the top of the scene, and the water blurs into a gentle veil of white, capturing the calm and peace of a rainforest after rain.

Inside is a light pencil sketch of the waterfall along with blank space for your own personal message, making this card suitable for any occasion you choose. It suits nature lovers, hikers, and anyone who finds comfort in quiet natural scenes.

Perfect for a birthday, thank you note, sympathy message, or simply to let someone know you are thinking of them. The tranquil imagery makes it a thoughtful choice whenever words alone are not quite enough.
