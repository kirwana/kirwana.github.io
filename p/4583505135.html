An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A canopy of golden maple leaves glows in autumn sunlight, with a bright starburst of sun breaking through the branches and soft, blurred forest trunks in the background. The warm yellow tones and sparkling light capture the quiet magic of a fall walk in the woods.

This card suits anyone who loves nature, autumn color, or simple woodland beauty. It works well as a thank you card, a just because note, a birthday greeting for a nature lover, or a warm hello sent during the fall season. The inside features a gentle pencil sketch of the same leafy scene, leaving plenty of room for your own handwritten message.

Perfect for gifting to gardeners, hikers, photographers, or anyone who appreciates the golden hush of autumn light filtering through the trees.
