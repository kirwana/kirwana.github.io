An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A vivid red rhododendron bloom glows in warm sunlight against a soft, blurred green background. The photograph captures every delicate vein and curled petal edge, with dark slender stamens reaching outward, celebrating the beauty of spring gardens in full color.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the same flower waits alongside blank space for your own personal message, making this card suitable for any occasion you choose. Send it as a birthday greeting, a thank you note, a just because hello, or a thoughtful gift for the gardener or nature lover in your life.

This card suits anyone who appreciates flowers, gardens and the natural world, from casual nature lovers to dedicated botanical enthusiasts. Its warm, cheerful tones make it a versatile choice for celebrating everyday moments and special milestones alike.

The card

A 150 mm square (5.9 in) card, folded at the side, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. At home it prints at 136 mm square (5.4 in) on A4 and 127 mm square (5 in) on US Letter, a little smaller than full size, because opened out it is wider than the paper; a print shop prints the full 150 mm square (5.9 in).

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 150 mm square (5.9 in) folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
