This striking black and white photograph captures the Sydney Harbour Bridge illuminated against the night sky, its iron arch reflected in the still waters below. The long exposure technique gives the city lights a soft glow and turns the harbour into a mirror of silver light, creating a dramatic and timeless scene.

With its high contrast tones and architectural grandeur, this image brings a bold urban statement to any room. It suits a modern living room, a home office, or a study, adding sophistication and a sense of place. The monochrome palette makes it easy to pair with most interior color schemes, from minimalist grey and white spaces to warm wood tones.

Perfect for lovers of cityscapes, architecture, and travel photography, this print also makes a thoughtful gift for anyone with a connection to Sydney or Australia. Whether displayed alone as a striking focal point or grouped with other black and white cityscapes, this piece brings depth, drama, and timeless elegance to your walls.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.
