An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A solitary volcanic peak rises above the arid highlands of the Bolivian altiplano, its snow dusted summit catching the light against a vast, empty sky. In the foreground, tufts of hardy desert grass punctuate the rocky plain, leading the eye toward distant hills and the silent giant beyond. Captured in striking black and white, this image distills the scene to its essential textures and contrasts, the smooth slopes of the mountain set against the wild, scattered brush below.

This print brings a sense of quiet vastness to any room. Its timeless monochrome palette makes it easy to place in a modern living room, a reading nook, a home office, or alongside travel and nature themed decor. The composition works well printed large as a striking statement piece or smaller as part of a gallery wall grouping.

A thoughtful gift for anyone who loves mountains, deserts, hiking, or South American landscapes, or for someone looking to bring a calm, dramatic focal point into their home or studio.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
