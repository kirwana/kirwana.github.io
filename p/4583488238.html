An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features a stunning night photograph of a historic stone church beneath a brilliant Milky Way, its stars scattered across a deep indigo sky above a quiet, rolling landscape. The soft glow on the horizon and the tiny cross beside the chapel add a peaceful, reflective feel to the scene.

Inside, a delicate pencil sketch of the same church awaits your personal message, leaving space to write your own words for any occasion. Its quiet, starlit beauty makes it a lovely choice for thank you notes, thinking of you cards, sympathy cards, or just because greetings.

Perfect for astronomy lovers, night sky photographers, travelers, and anyone who appreciates a peaceful landscape and a touch of wonder. This original photograph brings a serene, meditative mood to any greeting.

The card

A 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape card, folded along the top, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

The qr code on the back

The back of the card has a small QR code. Scanning it opens this same photograph as a large wall print in the shop: Lake Tekapo Church Print, a digital download to print at home or at a print shop, at sizes for framing. A nice extra for whoever receives the card.

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
