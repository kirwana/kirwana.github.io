An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original photograph of Cradle Mountain and Dove Lake in Tasmania, taken beneath a heavy, brooding sky. Lichen-covered boulders rise from still, glassy water in the foreground, their gold and green textures catching the light, while jagged peaks fade into the mist beyond the lake's calm surface.

Inside, a light pencil sketch echoes the scene, leaving plenty of open space for your own handwritten note. Its quiet, dramatic mood suits nature lovers, hikers and anyone who loves wild landscapes. Perfect for a birthday, thank you, thinking of you, or just because message, this card works equally well for a gentle sympathy note thanks to its calm, reflective tone.

A wonderful choice for outdoor enthusiasts, photographers, or anyone who appreciates the raw beauty of mountain scenery.
