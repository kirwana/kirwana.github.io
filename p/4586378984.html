Original photographs by Alan and Carolina, taken with real cameras. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

Stones of the Inca is a series of five photographs of ancient terraces and mountain ruins in the Peruvian Andes. It is a set of 5 prints designed to hang together. They share the same shape and scale, so they line up neatly on a wall as one gallery arrangement.

The whole set is about stone meeting mountain: dry-stone walls, stepped green terraces and weathered Inca masonry set against high Andean ridges. The palette is earthy and calm, with warm ochre stone, soft sage greens and slate blue skies. The mood is quiet and timeless, full of the scale and stillness of the high mountains.

Together these prints bring a sense of travel, history and wide open space to a room. They suit a living room feature wall, a hallway gallery or a study, and they sit comfortably with natural wood, linen and terracotta. They make a thoughtful gift for anyone who has walked the Sacred Valley or dreams of visiting Peru. They are a grounding choice for anyone who loves landscape photography and ancient places.

What you receive

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
