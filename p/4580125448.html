A tranquil black and white photograph of a wooden jetty stretching into perfectly still lake water, with a small platform bearing a weathered Shallow Water sign at its end. Silhouetted trees line the far shore beneath a soft, pale sky, creating a scene of quiet stillness and calm.

The long exposure smooths the water into glass, giving the image a meditative, almost dreamlike quality. The strong architectural lines of the dock lead the eye through the frame, balanced by the natural texture of the timber and the muted tones of the distant treeline.

This minimalist monochrome print suits a wide range of interiors, from coastal cottages to modern minimalist spaces. It brings a sense of calm and quiet sophistication to a living room, bedroom, office, or reading nook. Perfect for anyone who loves lake views, black and white photography, or serene nature scenes.

A wonderful gift for photography lovers, nature enthusiasts, or anyone wanting to add a peaceful focal point to their home decor.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

What you receive

Instant digital download. High resolution JPG files at 300 DPI covering a wide range of print sizes, in both inches and centimetres. Nothing is posted to you.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
