Original photographs by Alan and Carolina, taken with real cameras. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A matched set of two black and white desert mountain photographs, designed to hang together as one striking gallery moment. Both prints share the same dramatic high contrast style, deep inky skies, and sunlit ridgelines, so they line up neatly on a wall in shape and scale.

These images capture the raw, sculpted terrain of remote high altitude desert, where sharp light rakes across ancient slopes and volcanic peaks rise against near black skies. Scrubby tussocks and scattered stones in the foreground ground the compositions, while the mountains beyond feel almost lunar in their stillness and silence.

Together, this set brings a bold, contemplative mood to any room. The stark tonal range and clean geometry suit modern, minimalist, and rustic interiors alike, making them a natural fit for a living room, office, reading nook, or entryway gallery wall. Hung side by side, they create a sense of vast open landscape and quiet grandeur.

A striking choice for anyone drawn to travel, geology, or dramatic monochrome photography, and a thoughtful gift for nature lovers, hikers, or minimalist design enthusiasts.
