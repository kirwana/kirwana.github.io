An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original photograph of Bogota's historic skyline, where church towers and weathered clay-tile roofs rise against a moody, cloud-streaked sky. Domes and bell towers catch the late afternoon light while the old city spreads out below, capturing the layered charm of colonial architecture mixed with the modern buildings beyond.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the same scene awaits your own words, leaving plenty of room for a personal message. The blank interior makes this card wonderfully versatile, perfect as a just because card, a thank you note, or a thinking of you card for someone who loves travel, architecture, or cityscapes.

It also makes a lovely gift for anyone who has visited or dreamed of visiting Colombia, or for the traveler, photographer, or history lover in your life who appreciates beautiful old rooftops and dramatic skies.
