An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A breathtaking coastal sunrise unfolds over a rocky shoreline, where golden light breaks through dramatic clouds and spills across gentle waves. Weathered rocks dotted with barnacles line the wet sand, catching soft reflections of amber and rose hues as the tide sweeps around them in silky trails.

This tranquil seascape captures the quiet magic of early morning by the sea, with sunbeams piercing the horizon and painting the sky in warm gold, soft lavender, and deep blue. The long exposure creates a dreamy, flowing motion in the water, adding a sense of peaceful movement to the scene.

Perfect for a living room, bedroom, office, or coastal themed space, this print brings a calming, meditative energy to any wall. Its warm palette pairs beautifully with neutral, earthy, or blue interiors, making it a versatile addition to modern, minimalist, or beach house decor styles.

A wonderful gift for nature lovers, photographers, or anyone who finds peace by the ocean.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
