Original photographs by Alan and Carolina, taken with real cameras. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A matched set of 2 black and white photographs capturing the high altiplano of the Andes, where volcanic peaks meet still lagoons and wading flamingos move through shallow water. Designed to hang together, these prints share the same tonal palette, quiet stillness, and dramatic scale of mountain against sky, so they line up naturally on a wall as one cohesive body of work.

Across the set, the light is soft and clear, the compositions spacious and serene, letting rugged peaks and calm water take center stage. The flamingos add a quiet sense of life and movement without breaking the stillness of the scenery. Together the two prints read as a single visual story of remote wilderness, ideal for anyone drawn to minimalist landscape photography.

This set suits a living room, bedroom, office, or entryway gallery wall, especially in modern, Scandinavian, or nature inspired interiors. The monochrome tones make them easy to pair with existing decor while still making a striking visual statement. Whether displayed side by side or as part of a larger arrangement, these two prints bring a calm, sophisticated presence to any space, perfect for those who love mountains, wildlife, and serene natural scenery.
