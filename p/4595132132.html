A printable photo calendar of original photographs by Alan and Carolina: one photograph a month, printed at home or at a print shop.

Bring a year of blooms to your wall with this 2027 printable photo calendar. Each month has a large close-up flower photograph. The cover shows a pink everlasting daisy with a hoverfly resting on its golden centre. Inside are violet petals against fresh green leaves and bright sunflowers under a clear blue sky. The pictures are full of soft light and natural colour, and they bring a calm, cheerful feeling to every month. The clean, simple layout keeps the photographs centre stage and leaves plenty of room to write. It makes a thoughtful gift for gardeners, nature lovers and anyone who enjoys flowers, and it suits a kitchen, home office or study.
