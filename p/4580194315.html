An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A cascading waterfall tumbles over moss covered rock, framed by lush ferns and dense rainforest greenery. Long exposure photography renders the falling water as soft silky ribbons, creating a peaceful, dreamlike contrast against the textured stone and rich emerald foliage. The scene captures the quiet power and serenity found deep within a forest sanctuary.

This nature photograph brings a calming, organic energy to any space. The vivid greens and flowing white water pair beautifully with natural wood tones, neutral decor, or botanical themed rooms. It suits living rooms, bedrooms, home offices, meditation spaces, spa areas, and reading nooks, adding a sense of tranquility and connection to the outdoors.

A wonderful choice for nature lovers, hikers, or anyone who finds peace in the sound of running water. This print also makes a thoughtful gift for housewarmings, birthdays, or anyone drawn to lush green landscapes and the quiet beauty of hidden waterfalls.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
