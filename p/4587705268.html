An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

Morning light breaks over the Andes and pours through the ancient stone terraces of Machu Picchu in this black and white photograph. Beams of sunlight cut across the valley, catching the thatched Guardhouse perched on the terrace walls, while jagged peaks fade into layers of mist behind it. The rich tonal range, from deep shadowed stonework to glowing rays, gives the image a quiet, timeless drama.

This monochrome print brings a sense of calm and adventure to any space. It sits beautifully in a living room, study or hallway, and pairs easily with modern, minimalist, rustic or industrial decor. The neutral greys work with almost any colour scheme, making it a simple way to add depth to a gallery wall or anchor a large empty wall on its own.

A thoughtful gift for travellers, hikers and anyone who has walked the Inca Trail or dreams of visiting Peru, it is a lasting reminder of one of the great wonders of the world.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
