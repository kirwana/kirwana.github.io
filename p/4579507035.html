An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

Dove Lake stretches out beneath the jagged peaks of Cradle Mountain in this striking black and white photograph. A weathered boulder rises from the still water in the foreground, its rugged texture echoing the dramatic mountain range beyond. Soft clouds drift across the sky and mirror perfectly on the glassy lake surface, creating a serene sense of balance between earth, water, and sky.

This timeless monochrome scene brings a calm, contemplative energy to any space. The strong tonal contrast and natural composition make it a wonderful addition to a living room, office, or bedroom wall, pairing beautifully with both modern and rustic decor styles.

Perfect for nature lovers, hikers, and anyone drawn to the wild beauty of Tasmania, this print makes a thoughtful gift for outdoor enthusiasts or a meaningful piece for your own home gallery. Its quiet, majestic feel suits large statement walls as well as intimate reading corners, bringing the tranquility of the Tasmanian wilderness into your everyday surroundings.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look.
