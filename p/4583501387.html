An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A sunlit marigold opens in full golden bloom, its layered petals glowing against a shadowy backdrop of green leaves. Captured in warm natural light, this original photograph celebrates the simple beauty of the garden in close, vivid detail.

Inside, a gentle pencil sketch of the same flower awaits your own handwritten words, leaving plenty of space for a personal note. Because the inside is left open, this card suits nearly any occasion you choose: a birthday, a thank you, encouragement, or simply a just because hello to someone who loves flowers or gardening.

Perfect for nature lovers, gardeners, or anyone who appreciates a bright and cheerful floral image, this card brings a touch of sunshine to any greeting. Its warm colors and soft, artistic details make it a lovely keepsake for the recipient long after the occasion has passed.
