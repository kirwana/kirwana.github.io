An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original photograph of the dramatic Cliffs of Moher along Ireland's rugged Atlantic coastline. Towering green headlands rise above churning turquoise waves, with mist and moody storm clouds rolling in over the horizon, capturing the raw beauty and quiet power of this famous coastal landscape.

Inside you will find a light pencil sketch of the cliffs and open space to write your own personal message, making this card suitable for almost any occasion. It is a wonderful choice for nature lovers, hikers, travelers, and anyone who loves the sea. Send it as a thank you, a birthday greeting, a just because note, or a gentle thinking of you card to someone who dreams of wild landscapes and coastal adventures.

A striking piece of scenery that brings a sense of calm and grandeur to any greeting.

The card

A 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape card, folded along the top, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

The qr code on the back

The back of the card has a small QR code. Scanning it opens this same photograph as a large wall print in the shop: Cliffs of Moher Print, a digital download to print at home or at a print shop, at sizes for framing. A nice extra for whoever receives the card.

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
