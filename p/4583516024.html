This card features an original photograph of a lush forest waterfall cascading over moss covered rocks, framed by ferns and dappled greenery. The gentle golden pool at its base and soft, misty light give the image a calm, meditative feel perfect for sharing with someone who loves the outdoors.

Inside is a light pencil sketch of the same waterfall scene, with plenty of blank space for your own handwritten message. Because it is left open ended, this card works beautifully for birthdays, thank you notes, encouragement, sympathy, or simply to say you are thinking of someone.

A lovely choice for nature lovers, hikers, and anyone who finds peace in the sound of falling water. Send a little bit of the forest to a friend or family member who will appreciate the quiet beauty captured in this photograph.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

What you receive

Instant digital download, five files. Nothing is posted to you.
- Home print PDFs for A4 and US Letter, with cut and fold marks
- A print-shop PDF at full size with 1/8 in (3.2 mm) bleed
- JPG images for online card printers: the front, inside and back as separate images, as most card-printing sites ask for, plus the flat sheets
- A one-page printing guide

The card

A 5 × 7 in (12.7 × 17.8 cm) portrait card, folded at the side, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

The qr code on the back

The back of the card has a small QR code. Scanning it opens this same photograph as a large wall print in the shop: Waterfall Rainforest Print, a digital download to print at home or at a print shop, at sizes for framing. A nice extra for whoever receives the card.
etsy.com/listing/4580278958

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 5 × 7 in (12.7 × 17.8 cm) portrait folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
