This card features an original photograph of two striking amaryllis blooms, their white petals brushed with fine red veining and edged in soft crimson, set against a lush green garden backdrop. The delicate detail of the petals and golden stamens brings a fresh, botanical charm to any occasion.

Inside is a light pencil sketch of the same flowers, leaving space for your own handwritten message. Because it is left open ended, this card suits birthdays, thank you notes, get well wishes, or simply a just because greeting to brighten someone's day.

Perfect for gardeners, nature lovers, and anyone who appreciates fine floral photography, this card makes a thoughtful, personal gift for friends and family who love flowers and the beauty of the natural world.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

What you receive

Instant digital download, five files. Nothing is posted to you.
- Home print PDFs for A4 and US Letter, with cut and fold marks

- JPG images for online card printers: the front, inside and back as separate images, as most card-printing sites ask for, plus the flat sheets
- A one-page printing guide

The card

A 150 mm square (5.9 in) card, folded at the side, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. At home it prints at 136 mm square (5.4 in) on A4 and 127 mm square (5 in) on US Letter, a little smaller than full size, because opened out it is wider than the paper; a print shop prints the full 150 mm square (5.9 in).

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 150 mm square (5.9 in) folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.

About the photos

The table scenes are mockups to show the card; the props are not included.

Colour
