An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A hoverfly rests on a cluster of delicate white spring flowers in this original close-up photograph. Its black and yellow striped body and lace-like wings stand out against soft petals with sunny yellow centres. Behind them, a blurred green garden background holds a few gentle hints of pink.

The colours are fresh and bright, and the photograph has the calm, sunlit feel of a quiet morning in the garden. Inside, a light pencil sketch of the scene sits beside plenty of space for your own words.

The card is left blank, so it works for many occasions. Send it for a birthday, as a thank you, or just because. It would suit gardeners, wildlife watchers and anyone who loves bees, insects and wildflowers, and it makes a thoughtful note for a nature lover in spring or summer.
