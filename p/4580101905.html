A striking night view of Sydney Harbour, framed beneath the sweeping steel arch of the Harbour Bridge with the iconic Opera House glowing softly in the distance. Warm amber light spills across the historic stone wall and iron fence in the foreground, contrasting beautifully with the deep indigo sky and shimmering water below.

This image captures the energy and elegance of one of the world's most recognizable skylines, blending architecture, history and atmosphere into a single striking composition. The interplay of warm and cool tones makes it a versatile piece for a range of interiors.

Perfect for a living room, office, hallway or study, this print adds a sophisticated urban touch to any space. It works well in modern, industrial or contemporary decor styles, and makes a thoughtful gift for travelers, architecture lovers or anyone drawn to iconic cityscapes.
