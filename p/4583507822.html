An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original photograph of a stone lighthouse standing guard at the edge of a rocky harbor, its red lantern room glowing against a moody, cloud streaked sky. Sailboats rest in the marina beyond, and a distant coastal town rises along the hills, capturing the quiet drama of a working seaside harbor at dusk.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the lighthouse offers a charming touch alongside blank space for your own handwritten note, making this card suitable for nearly any occasion or none at all.

Perfect for lighthouse enthusiasts, sailors, nature lovers, and anyone drawn to coastal scenery, this card works beautifully as a thank you note, a just because gesture, a birthday greeting, or a thoughtful way to say thinking of you to someone who loves the sea and dramatic skies.
