Original photographs by Alan and Carolina, taken with real cameras. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A pair of photographs of glacial ice and mountain snow, these prints are a set of 2 designed to hang together. They share the same shape and scale, so they line up neatly side by side on a wall.

Together they capture the raw drama of high mountain ice: fractured seracs in pale glacial blue, fresh snow over dark rock, and soft overcast light that keeps every crease and crevasse crisp. The palette is cool and restrained, with icy blues, clean whites and charcoal greys, giving the set a calm yet powerful mood.

These prints bring a sense of space and quiet to a living room, bedroom or home office, and suit minimalist, Scandinavian and modern interiors where a cool, airy palette works well. Hung as a pair above a sofa, bed or console table, they make a gallery wall that feels considered rather than busy.

They make a thoughtful gift for hikers, mountaineers, travellers and anyone who loves wild alpine landscapes and the natural world.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
