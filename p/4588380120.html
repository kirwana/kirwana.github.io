An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A lone volcano rises above the high Bolivian altiplano in this black and white landscape photograph. Tufts of wild ichu grass catch the hard mountain light across a stony plain, while the peak stands pale against a deep, almost black sky. The scene is quiet and spacious. The stark contrast of high altitude air gives every rock and blade of grass a crisp, sculpted edge.

The wide panoramic format leads the eye from the textured grasses in the foreground, across the open desert, to the mountain on the horizon. It creates a strong sense of distance and calm.

This print brings stillness and scale to a room. It sits naturally above a sofa, bed or sideboard. Its monochrome palette works with almost any colour scheme, from warm wood and natural textures to clean modern interiors. It suits a living room, bedroom, study or hallway, and makes a thoughtful gift for travellers, hikers and anyone who has fallen for the Andes or the wild places of South America.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
