A lone volcano rises above the high Bolivian altiplano in this black and white landscape photograph. Tufts of wild ichu grass catch the hard mountain light across a stony plain, while the peak stands pale against a deep, almost black sky. The scene is quiet and spacious. The stark contrast of high altitude air gives every rock and blade of grass a crisp, sculpted edge.

The wide panoramic format leads the eye from the textured grasses in the foreground, across the open desert, to the mountain on the horizon. It creates a strong sense of distance and calm.
