A printable photo calendar of original photographs by Alan and Carolina: one photograph a month, printed at home or at a print shop.

Spend 2027 in the high Andes with twelve original black and white photographs, one for each month. Volcanic peaks rise over still lagoons where flamingos wade and the water mirrors the slopes. Snow-dusted ridges sit beneath bright cumulus clouds, and cracked salt flats run to the horizon. The monochrome tones make for a calm, timeless year with plenty of space and quiet. It suits anyone who loves mountains, travel, South America or fine art photography, and it makes a thoughtful gift for hikers, travelers and photography lovers.
