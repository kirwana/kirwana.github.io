An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A honeybee hovers beside a delicate pale pink blossom, its wings caught mid flutter as it gathers pollen in warm spring light. Soft blurred branches and hints of pink fill the background, giving the photograph a gentle, sunlit feel that captures the quiet energy of the season.

This card suits nature lovers, gardeners and anyone who appreciates the small wonders of spring. It works beautifully as a birthday card, a thank you note, or a just because card to brighten someone's day. The blank inside, featuring a light pencil sketch of the bee and blossom, leaves plenty of room for your own personal message.

Whether sent to a beekeeper, a flower enthusiast, or simply someone who loves the outdoors, this card brings a touch of nature's beauty and gentle warmth to any occasion.
