This card features an original close-up photograph of a peacock, its iridescent blue neck and emerald feathers glowing against a soft, dreamy green background. The bird's crest and gentle gaze give the image a calm, elegant feel that suits nature lovers and bird enthusiasts alike.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the peacock adds a handmade touch, with plenty of blank space for your own personal message. Because the inside is left open, this card works beautifully for birthdays, thank you notes, encouragement, or simply a just because hello to someone who loves wildlife and beautiful color.

Whether you are sending it to a bird lover, a nature enthusiast, or someone who simply appreciates striking photography, this peacock card brings a bit of natural elegance to any occasion, personal or celebratory.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

The card

A 150 mm square (5.9 in) card, folded at the side, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. At home it prints at 136 mm square (5.4 in) on A4 and 127 mm square (5 in) on US Letter, a little smaller than full size, because opened out it is wider than the paper; a print shop prints the full 150 mm square (5.9 in).

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 150 mm square (5.9 in) folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
