Original photographs by Alan and Carolina, taken with real cameras. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A pair of black and white photographs of the Inca stronghold high in the Peruvian Andes, sold as a set of 2 prints designed to hang together. They share the same shape and scale, so they line up neatly side by side or stacked on a wall.

These prints treat the ancient citadel and its mountains in rich monochrome. Sharp granite peaks rise over terraces and stone walls, and soft light falls across the ridges and through the haze of the cloud forest. Without colour, the set leans on texture and tone: deep shadow, bright stone, and layer after layer of mountain fading into the distance. The mood is quiet, timeless and a little dramatic, more like a memory of the place than a holiday snapshot.

Hung together, they bring a calm sense of scale to a living room, study, hallway or office. The neutral greys sit easily with modern, minimalist, rustic or industrial interiors and work well alongside wood, linen and black metal. The set makes a thoughtful gift for travellers who have walked the Inca Trail, lovers of history and archaeology, or anyone drawn to mountain landscapes.
