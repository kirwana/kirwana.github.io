An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features a striking black and white photograph of a dahlia in full bloom, its layered petals catching the light with delicate droplets of dew still clinging to their surface. The dark background makes the flower glow, giving the image a moody, timeless quality that feels both elegant and intimate.

Inside is a light pencil sketch of the same dahlia, leaving space for your own handwritten message. Because the card carries no printed greeting, it suits nearly any occasion, a birthday, thank you note, thinking of you card, or simply a thoughtful just because gesture.

It is a lovely choice for flower lovers, gardeners and anyone who appreciates fine art photography. The rich contrast and natural detail make it a keepsake worthy of framing after the message inside has been read and treasured.
