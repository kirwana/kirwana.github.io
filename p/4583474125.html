An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card showcases a striking black and white photograph of the Sydney Harbour Bridge illuminated at night, its steel arch glowing against a dark sky while the city skyline glimmers in the distance and the harbour waters reflect the scene in soft, silvery light.

Inside, a light pencil sketch of the bridge greets the recipient, leaving plenty of room for your own personal message. The timeless, dramatic image makes this card perfect for architecture and travel lovers, city dwellers, or anyone who appreciates fine art photography.

Its versatile design suits birthdays, thank you notes, congratulations, or a simple just because greeting. Whether sent to a fan of urban landscapes or someone who loves iconic landmarks, this elegant card brings a touch of nighttime magic to any occasion worth celebrating.
