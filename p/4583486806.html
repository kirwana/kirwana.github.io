An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original photograph of a golden sunset glowing over rugged sandstone cliffs and a calm coastal sea. Warm amber light washes across the sky and headlands while gentle waves break along the rocky shore below, capturing a quiet, peaceful moment by the ocean.

Inside you will find a light pencil sketch of the same cliff and coastline, along with open space to write your own personal message, making this card suited to any occasion you choose.

This design is perfect for nature lovers, bird lovers, beach walkers and anyone who appreciates coastal scenery and golden hour light. It works beautifully as a thank you card, a just because card, a birthday card for someone who loves the sea, or a thinking of you card for a friend who treasures quiet landscapes and travel memories.
