This piece captures the historic skyline of Bogota, Colombia, where weathered church bell towers and a domed cathedral rise above a sea of terracotta rooftops. Storm clouds roll across the sky, catching the last warm light of day and casting a golden glow over the old town below, while modern high rises peek out in the distance, blending centuries of history in one view.

The rich rust and clay tones of the rooftops paired with the dramatic grey sky make this a striking statement piece for any room. It brings warmth and old world character to a living room, home office, reading nook, or entryway, and works beautifully alongside travel themed decor or other cityscape prints.

Perfect for lovers of architecture, travel photography, and Latin American culture, this print makes a thoughtful gift for anyone who has visited Colombia or dreams of exploring its historic capital. Pair it with neutral or wood toned frames to let the warm colors take center stage on your wall.

About the photograph

An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

About the photos

The room photographs are mockups. Frames, mats, furniture and accessories are not included and are shown only to suggest how the print might look. The print is sized to approximate scale in each room, but it is an illustration, not a measurement. Please measure your own wall and choose the size that suits it.
