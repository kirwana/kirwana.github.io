An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A dramatic black and white photograph of the Sydney Opera House captured beneath a moody, cloud filled sky. Light rakes across the iconic sail shaped roofs while the promenade below hums with people strolling and dining along the harbour. The contrast between the storm above and the glowing architecture below gives this image a striking, cinematic feel.

This card suits architecture and travel lovers, fans of Sydney and Australia, or anyone who appreciates fine art photography. Its timeless monochrome style makes it a wonderful choice for a birthday, thank you note, congratulations, or simply to say hello. Inside is a light pencil sketch of the Opera House with open space for your own handwritten message, making it a versatile keepsake for any occasion or recipient who loves iconic landmarks and city skylines.

THE CARD
A 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape card, folded along the top, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. It prints at full size on A4 or US Letter card.

PRINTING
At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 7 × 5 in (17.8 × 12.7 cm) landscape folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
