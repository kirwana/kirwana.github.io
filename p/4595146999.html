A printable photo calendar of original photographs by Alan and Carolina: one photograph a month, printed at home or at a print shop.

Spend 2027 in the high Andes of Bolivia with twelve original landscape photographs. On the cover, the white salt crust of the Salar de Uyuni cracks into hexagons under a deep blue sky, with a distant volcano on the horizon. Other months show golden grasses, rugged mountain slopes and wide, quiet altiplano views. The mood is calm, bright and spacious, a year of clear air and open space. Each month uses a large photo layout with a clean grid that starts on Sunday, and US federal holidays are marked. It makes a thoughtful gift for travelers, South America lovers, hikers and anyone who has dreamed of standing on the salt flats.
