An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

This card features an original photograph of cascading wisteria blossoms, their delicate white and lavender petals glowing in soft sunlight against a warm, blurred background. The image captures the gentle, romantic beauty of spring in full bloom, perfect for anyone who loves flowers, gardens or nature.

Inside you will find a light pencil sketch of the wisteria blooms along with plenty of room to add your own personal message. Because the inside is left open, this card works beautifully for many occasions, including birthdays, thank you notes, thinking of you moments, or simply saying hello to someone special.

Whether you are celebrating a milestone or just want to brighten someone's day, this wisteria card brings a touch of natural elegance to any greeting. It suits gardeners, flower lovers, and anyone who appreciates the quiet beauty of blossoms in spring light.

The card

A 150 mm square (5.9 in) card, folded at the side, with a pencil sketch and room inside for your own message. At home it prints at 136 mm square (5.4 in) on A4 and 127 mm square (5 in) on US Letter, a little smaller than full size, because opened out it is wider than the paper; a print shop prints the full 150 mm square (5.9 in).

Printing

At home: 200 to 300 gsm card, printed at Actual size (100%), two-sided, flipping on the short edge; then cut on the marks, score and fold. At a print shop: upload the print-shop PDF and order a 150 mm square (5.9 in) folded card, scored, on 300 to 350 gsm card. With an online card printer: upload the front, inside and back images from the zip; its README says which is which.
