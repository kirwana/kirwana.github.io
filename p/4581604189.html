Original photographs by Alan and Carolina, taken with real cameras. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A series of four macro flower photographs sold together as a matched set. Each captures delicate petals bathed in soft sunlight, from cool lavender and blush tones to warm golden centers, framed in the same close crop and square scale so they line up beautifully on a wall together.

Together these prints celebrate the quiet detail of blooms in bright, natural light. The palette moves gently between soft purples, creamy whites and touches of yellow, while the blurred green backgrounds keep the mood calm and airy. They read as a coherent body of work rather than four unrelated pictures, making them easy to hang as a grid or a row.

This set suits a bedroom, reading nook or living room where you want a touch of botanical charm without heavy color. They also make a thoughtful gift for gardeners, nature lovers or anyone who appreciates flowers and soft, sunlit photography. Hang them together for a gallery wall moment, or space them out for a subtler botanical thread through your home.
