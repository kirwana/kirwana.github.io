An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A vivid close-up photograph of a red and yellow pansy in full bloom, its velvety petals streaked with deep burgundy veins, set against a soft green garden background. The warm sunlight highlights every delicate detail of this cheerful flower, making it a joyful piece of nature photography.

This card suits any occasion where a burst of color and natural beauty feels right, from birthdays to thank you notes to a simple just because greeting. It is especially fitting for gardeners, flower lovers, and anyone who appreciates the quiet charm of a blooming pansy.

Inside, a gentle pencil sketch of the same flower leaves space for your own personal message, making this card a versatile and heartfelt choice for friends and family who love flowers and the outdoors.
