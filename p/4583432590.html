An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera, made into a folded greeting card you print yourself.

A vivid close-up photograph of bright red blossoms unfurling on a slender branch, their golden stamens glowing in warm sunlight against a soft green background. The delicate curl of the petals and the tiny red bud beneath them capture a fleeting, joyful moment in nature.

This card suits nature lovers, gardeners and anyone who appreciates fine botanical photography. Its cheerful color and open, welcoming design make it a lovely choice for birthdays, thank you notes, get well wishes or simply saying hello. Because the inside is left blank apart from a gentle pencil sketch of the flower, you can add your own personal message for any occasion.

Bring a bit of vibrant garden color to someone's day with this original floral photograph, perfect for spring celebrations or a simple thoughtful gesture any time of year.
