An original photograph by Alan and Carolina, taken with a real camera. Every size listed prints sharp, and the larger formats are best viewed from around two feet, the distance a framed print is normally seen from.

A striking black and white photograph capturing a flock of flamingos wading through the mirrored shallows of a high altiplano lagoon, with a towering volcano rising behind them under a deep, dramatic sky. The stillness of the water perfectly reflects both the birds and the mountain, creating a serene symmetry that feels both vast and intimate.

This image blends the quiet elegance of wildlife photography with the raw grandeur of landscape art, making it a striking addition to living rooms, offices, reading nooks, or any space that calls for a touch of natural drama. The high contrast tones and timeless monochrome palette allow it to complement both modern minimalist interiors and more classic, earthy decor styles.

Whether you are drawn to it for its serene subject matter or its powerful composition, this piece brings a sense of calm wilderness into any room. It also makes a thoughtful gift for travelers, nature lovers, bird watchers, or anyone who appreciates fine art photography with a story to tell.
